# Chapter 14 — Content Series

The blog runs three named series alongside one-off posts. Each series has a slug-prefix convention, a category, and a defined source-material pipeline, so anyone picking up the editorial calendar knows where the next post comes from without re-reading old issues.

## Made with FontLab

**Slug prefix:** `made-with-fontlab-{designer-slug}`
**Category:** `Made with FontLab`
**Cadence:** roughly one per quarter, 6–8 entries spanning 2024–2026.

Each entry profiles **one** type designer — biography, the fonts they shipped, the FontLab features that mattered to them, one or two quotes if reachable. No omnibus listicles. The 2024-08-12 and 2024-11-19 anchor posts are converted from listicles into series-anchor pages that link out to per-designer entries.

Authoring rules:

- 600–1200 words.
- Open with one of their fonts in the wild — billboard, book jacket, app — not with biography.
- Include 2–4 specimens (downloaded from foundry sites with permission, or stills from the designer's specimen PDF).
- Link to the designer's own foundry; never embed a sales widget.
- End with a single "Browse their fonts on {foundry}" call-to-action — styled as the standard Read-More button.

Sourcing the designer list: Slovak/Czech, Polish, Russian, German, Brazilian and US designers are over-represented in the FontLab user base; the roster in `spec/13.md` reflects that. Each profile must verify the designer used FontLab (about page, interview, or public statement) before publication; if the link is unprovable, mark the slot `TBD: confirm FontLab attribution`.

## FontLab TV

**Slug prefix:** `fontlab-tv-{nn}-{topic}` where `nn` is the Kapusta-chapter number.
**Category:** `FontLab TV`
**Cadence:** ~1 per fortnight throughout 2025–2026; 25 chapters in the Kapusta course are the long tail.

Each post is the written companion to one chapter of the FontLab × A. Kapusta YouTube series (`reference/fontlab-tv/dev/www.fontlab.tv/yt/playlists.yaml`). Structure:

1. One-paragraph "what this chapter teaches" lead.
2. Embedded YouTube playlist (lazy-loaded iframe).
3. Numbered list of episode titles, each linking to the timestamp on YouTube.
4. A "Three things that surprised us" pull-out — the editorial value-add over the raw video.
5. A "Read more" button to the relevant chapter of `help.fontlab.com`.

Older FontLab TV one-offs (Matthew Carter webinar 2014, Brush Romans webinar 2014, Color Fonts tutorial 2014, Sketch-to-Font ScanFont 2019, etc.) are written in the same format but without the chapter number; slug is `fontlab-tv-{topic}`.

## Tutorials

**Slug prefix:** `tutorials-{briem|calfonts}-{topic}`
**Category:** `Tutorials`
**Cadence:** ~1 per month, paired across 2024–2026.

These modernize the long-form content under `reference/fldoc/src/fontlab/8/md/tutorials/briem/` and `.../calfonts/`. Each post takes ONE briem chapter (`4-2-bold`, `4-3-italic`, `4-4-scans`, `5-0-spacing`, `6-0-elbow-grease`, `7-0-glossary`) or one calfonts cluster (Drawing, Honing, Spacing, Italics, Variations) and:

- Trims the 1990s-era prose; keeps the diagrams.
- Adds a "What changed in FontLab 8" sidebar.
- Sources figure images from `reference/fldoc/.../briem/img/` and `.../calfonts/img/`.
- Closes with the standard Read-More button to the full tutorial on `help.fontlab.com`.

The first tutorial post (`2023-04-17-briem-foundations`, already shipped) is the series anchor. Subsequent posts are tagged `tutorials` and linked from the anchor.

## Series category & nav surfacing

All three series are in `categories_allowed` (see `spec/07.md`) and reachable at:

- `/category/made-with-fontlab/`
- `/category/fontlab-tv/`
- `/category/tutorials/`

The local Web Component menu does **not** add series links — the sidebar would crowd. Instead, the site's `index.md` lead paragraph mentions the series and links to the three category pages.

## Pull-out boxes

Each series uses a MaterialX admonition shorthand for visual consistency:

- **Made with FontLab:** `!!! quote "Made with FontLab"` — Crimson Pro pull-quote.
- **FontLab TV:** `!!! example "Watch the chapter"` — playlist embed.
- **Tutorials:** `!!! tip "What changed in FontLab 8"` — sidebar callout.

Conventions are defined once in `src_docs/md/css/extra.css`; authors do not redefine them per post.
