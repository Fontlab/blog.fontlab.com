# Chapter 1 — Goals & Non-Goals

## What this site is

blog.fontlab.com is a technical publishing venue for people who work with type. The primary audience is type designers and FontLab users — people who care about OpenType features, variable fonts, colour font formats, and the craft of making letters. The secondary audience is anyone adjacent to that world: font developers, typographers who want to understand what their tools are actually doing.

The editorial mission is simple: publish things that are still worth reading in two years. No release announcements. No sale countdowns. No "we're excited to share" anything. Just clear technical writing, occasionally opinionated, occasionally funny.

## What this site is not

**Not a product changelog.** FontLab 8.3.2 fixing a kerning display bug is not a blog post. The help site and the app's built-in release notes handle that.

**Not a documentation mirror.** If something belongs in `help.fontlab.com`, it goes there. Blog posts can link to documentation, but they do not replace it.

**Not a marketing channel.** No "limited time offer" posts, no affiliate content, no SEO bait. The old WordPress export in `reference/fontlab-com-oldpub/` contains plenty of that material; it stays retired.

**Not a news feed.** Industry news, conference reports, and link roundups are out of scope. The blog is not a substitute for a newsletter or social media.

## Audience profile

The reader arrived here because they care about type. They probably know what a glyph is. They may or may not know what a GSUB lookup is, but they are willing to find out. They are comfortable with technical depth and hostile to vagueness. They read slowly and expect the writing to repay that slowness.

Posts should assume literacy in type design fundamentals and explain everything else. A post about variable font axes should not explain what a font is. A post about the `COLR` table can assume the reader has heard of OpenType.

## Editorial scope

In scope:
- Technical explainers: OpenType, variable fonts, colour fonts, Unicode, kerning, spacing
- How-to posts: specific FontLab workflows explained with enough detail to follow
- Historical and conceptual pieces: the 2013 colour fonts article is the template here
- Opinions on type tooling, format design, industry standards — signed by a named author

Out of scope:
- Product announcements (covered by fontlab.com itself)
- Point-release notes (covered by the help site)
- Promo/sale posts (retired; see [chapter 10](10.md) for porting decisions)
- Tutorials that duplicate the help site word-for-word

## Success criterion

A post is good if someone finds it via search two years from now and immediately understands whether it answers their question. That is the only metric that matters.
