# Editorial expansion from issue 203 drafts

## Provenance

In May 2026, five LLMs (Grok x2, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Qwen, Gemini, GPT) were asked to produce candidate blog posts for blog.fontlab.com tied to FontLab 8, TransType 4, and Vexy Lines, with sources in twelve languages. The seven raw responses are in `issues/draft-posts/40[1-7]-*.md`. They overlap heavily — every model wrote a "rescue legacy Type 1 with TransType" angle, every model wrote a "variable fonts on the web" angle. One response (`405-qwen.md`) is meta-research with no usable post drafts and is excluded.

This chapter cherry-picks the most distinct **35 post slots** from the remaining drafts. Quality cap: each slot below has (a) a clear FontLab / TransType / Vexy Lines tie, (b) at least one non-promotional source URL in its draft, and (c) at least one viable image candidate. Drafts that failed all three were dropped.

These slots **supplement, not replace** the 60-post roadmap in `spec/13.md`. Where a date here collides with an existing scheduled post in `spec/13.md`, treat the issue-203 slot as the higher-priority candidate to fill the next open TBD slot in that month.

## Distribution

- **2024:** 10 posts (Feb, Mar, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec) — TransType-anchored and timeless typography pieces (TransType 4 is the historical hook); FontLab 8 process pieces.
- **2025:** 18 posts spread Jan–Dec — variable-fonts, OpenType, multilingual; Vexy Lines posts only from December.
- **2026:** 7 posts Jan–May — forward-looking trends, motion, kinetic type, color-font follow-ups, Vexy Lines deeper dives.

Total: **35**. Vexy Lines posts honor the Dec 2025 release date constraint. TransType 4 posts cluster around its 2013 release and the 2023 Adobe Type 1 cutoff, but several are dated 2024 because the conversion-pain narrative is what's evergreen.

## Author legend

- `adam` — Adam Twardoch, general typographic/process pieces.
- `fontlab` — FontLab-app-specific posts (FontLab 8 features, OpenType, drawing tools).
- `transtype` — TransType-app-specific posts.
- `vexy-lines` — Vexy Lines-app-specific posts.

## Tier legend

VF = Variable Fonts · OT = OpenType · VEC = Vector / Vexy · TT = TransType / Web Fonts · TD = Typography & Type Design · MUL = Multilingual / Non-Latin · VL = Vexy Lines

## Quality gates per post

Before a post is moved out of TODO into a real `src_docs/md/posts/<date>-<slug>.md` draft, it must pass:

1. **Tie test** — the FontLab/TransType/Vexy-Lines connection is in the first three paragraphs, not bolted on at the end.
2. **Source test** — at least three non-promotional reference URLs in the trailing list, mirroring the format of existing posts in `src_docs/md/posts/`.
3. **Image test** — at least one in-repo media file under `src_docs/md/media/<slug>/`. No hot-linked production images.
4. **Voice test** — leads with a strong first line; lower-case sentence-case titles; proper typographic punctuation; no "revolutionary"-class hype words.
5. **Date test** — Vexy Lines posts dated 2025-12-01 or later; TransType posts that anchor to the 2013 release say so explicitly; FontLab 8 posts plausible for 2024–2026.
6. **No-duplicate test** — slug doesn't collide with anything in `ls src_docs/md/posts/`. Slugs above were checked against the existing roster and none collide.

## How this slots into spec/13.md

`spec/13.md` reserves date slots for "Made with FontLab", "FontLab TV", "Tutorials" series and leaves several months with TBD or open slots. Where a date in the table above matches a TBD in `spec/13.md`, the issue-203 slot fills it. Where it collides with an already-named post, the issue-203 slot moves to the next open day in the same month. The dates above were chosen avoiding the existing 2024–2026 posts in `src_docs/md/posts/`.
