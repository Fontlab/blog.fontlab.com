# Chapter 13 — Editorial Roadmap 2024–2026

Sixty planned posts: 12 in 2026 (Jan–May, up to 2026-05-07), 24 in 2025, 24 in 2024. Already-shipped slugs (see `src_docs/md/posts/`) are not duplicated here; the slots below add to that backlog so 2024–2026 are tightly packed with FontLab content.

Source pools:

- **TV-K{NN}** = FontLab × A. Kapusta chapter `NN` from `reference/fontlab-tv/dev/www.fontlab.tv/yt/playlists.yaml` (25 chapters available).
- **Briem-{ID}** = `reference/fldoc/src/fontlab/8/md/tutorials/briem/{ID}/`.
- **Calfonts-{ID}** = `reference/fldoc/src/fontlab/8/md/tutorials/calfonts/{ID}/`.
- **MWF-{designer}** = "Made with FontLab" series profile (one per post; see `spec/14.md`).
- **FL-help** = curated summary of a `help.fontlab.com` topic.
- **Old-pub** = a not-yet-ported post from `reference/fontlab-com-oldpub/`.

Where a slot is genuinely undecided, the cell reads `TBD: select topic from <source>`.

## Done-criteria for the roadmap

A planned slot is "done" when the post is merged with `draft: false` and the `date.created` matches the date in the table. A slot may slip ±14 days without re-issuing the spec; bigger slips warrant an update commit on this file. `TBD: confirm FontLab attribution` slots must be either confirmed or replaced before drafting begins — never publish an unverified attribution.
