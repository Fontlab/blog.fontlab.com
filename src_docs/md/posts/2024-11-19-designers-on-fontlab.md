What do working type designers actually think of FontLab 8? The most substantive testimonials — the ones that say *why*, not just *that*.

<!-- more -->

---

> The **definitive** FontLab version to have: blazingly **fast**, with **superbly rich** functionality that touches almost every aspect of type design. The FontLab team kept all the good aspects of previous versions, polished the rough edges, and built on top of that.
>
> The **best vector engine** for drawing and manipulation I have seen in ages. Rock-steady **interpolation** engine, compliant with variable OpenType fonts. An excellent multi-paradigm approach — old-school outlines, element references, components, auto-generated glyphs, or all combined. Auto layers, auto OpenType feature generation, a super powerful **Python**-based API. And **multi-platform**: a fact I consider very important.
>
> The new FontLab is an **endless ocean** of opportunities — an app for every taste and workflow.

**Vassil Kateliev**, [Karandash type foundry](http://karandash.eu/), developer of TypeRig

---

> Having used so many font development products for more than 27 years, I can say **without hesitation** that FontLab 8 is the **best and most comprehensive** font making and editing program ever made. Its customizability and wealth of features set a very high bar for adaptability in simple, complex, or automated environments. Add a **positively responsive** development team and a **helpful** support community, and FontLab 8 should be the **primary** tool in every type designer's daily workflow.

**Patrick Griffin**, [Canada Type](https://canadatype.com/)

---

> The major advantage we appreciate in FontLab is its **highly integrated** approach — no extensive plugin maintenance required, yet open to **creative** and sometimes **unconventional** workflows. Prototyping speed has **greatly improved** due to more accessible ways of translating design variables into interpolation patterns, the **remarkably flexible** system of **masters**, masks, and **service layers**, and **effortless** data movement between source documents.
>
> FontLab 8 **excels in new features and innovations**. While still in beta, we tried the new skeleton-based design tools in developing a Hebrew typeface, and were **pretty amazed** by what is possible.

**Robert Strauch & Alexander Haberer**, [robertstrauch.de](https://www.robertstrauch.de) — designers of corporate typefaces for BMW Group and KUKA AG

---

> FontLab is powerful enough to work **from start to finish** inside one program. The new **Stroke** panel and **Thickness** tool are a revelation. There had been much talk about using "outline skeletons" — FontLab 8 is the **first app** to put that into practice in an **integrated** way.
>
> Add the features from FontLab 7 — **elements** for building from blocks, **fractional** coordinates, fast **automatic OpenType features** — and you have the **most precise font creator** available.
>
> Without FontLab 8, I could do **just about nothing**.

**Dave Lawrence**, [California Type Foundry](https://calfonts.com)

---

> **Rock-solid** font development software, from **design** to **engineering**. FontLab has the **best drawing tools** I've ever seen, and they just **got better**. **FontAudit** keeps you in check. The new views for masks and layers are super-handy for multiple masters; the **nudging** workflow is spot-on.
>
> FontLab 8 is the **juiciest release** of FontLab. And it's **running flawlessly** on my Linux machine.

**Fábio Duarte Martins**, [Scannerlicker](https://fonts.scannerlicker.net/)

---

> There are many **drawing** tools in the new FontLab, each offering **different ways** to start an outline. Editing is **intuitive**. **Power Nudge** adjusts locally related nodes **in harmony**. **Tunni Lines** and **Smart Corners** give **fluid control** over the outline. The **flexibility** of the drawing tools continues to **impress**.

**Jeremy Tankard**, [typography.net](https://typography.net/), designer of Bliss and Corbel

---

> I've been using FontLab for over 20 years, not only for font design but for **complex vector drawing**. Especially since version 7, it does **a much better job** than other major vector editing software.

**Eyal Holtzman**, designer of [Kristal](https://www.boldmonday.com/typefaces/kristal/) at Bold Monday

---

> No other type design software can match the **speed** and **reliability** of the **drawing interface** of the new FontLab.

**Ramiro Espinoza**, [ReType Foundry](http://www.re-type.com/), designer of Barbieri and Medusa

---

> FontLab is **much more advanced** than the competition. I couldn't live without **Genius** nodes, **Quick measurement**, and **Smart corners**.

**Tobias Kvant**, [Kvant Art Direction](https://kvant.org/)
