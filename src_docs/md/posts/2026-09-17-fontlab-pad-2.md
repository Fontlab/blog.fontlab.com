A variable font holds hundreds of styles, a color font brings its own palette, and a Designspace with UFO masters holds a design that has not been compiled into a font at all. Your document app shows you what it supports, which is rarely all of that.

FontLab Pad 2 is the room where you get the text right first. Open a font file or an installed font. Type. Choose an instance, or move the axis sliders. Copy as Bitmap, SVG or PDF, or export a PNG, SVG or PDF file to place in your design.

## Variable and multiple-master fonts

Pad previews native variable OpenType fonts at their named instances and at custom axis locations. It also interpolates sources: Glyphs files, Designspace documents with linked UFO masters, and multiple-master Type 1 fonts. If a source has no predefined instances, you choose masters instead.

Predefined instances live in the sidebar. Pick one, or adjust the axes with sliders or numeric values: your preview text and formatting stay as they are while the design moves under them.

The header shows the family name for variable fonts and the current style or axis coordinates, so you always know which style you are looking at. The instance and formatting panels share the sidebar, and clicking the style selector in the header shows or hides the instance panel.

## Glyphs by name

Press the `/` key in the preview and type a glyph name or a character to insert any variant: a ligature, a swash, an alternate. Glyphs in the picker are drawn with the current instance, so what you choose is what you get.

Placeholder glyphs now use nonzero winding fill, so overlapping outlines stay filled instead of punching holes in each other.

## Copying, with the format you meant

The Copy button is its own control now, and it offers Bitmap, SVG and PDF. It remembers the format you picked last, and it preserves the font color and the preview layout. If you set your text over a transparent background to check it against a design, that is what lands in the other app.

## What Pad opens

Static and variable OpenType, color OpenType in SVG, `sbix`, `COLR`/`CPAL` and `CBDT`/`CBLC` flavors, Type 1 and multiple-master fonts, WOFF web fonts, Photofont, SVG fonts, UFO, Designspace with linked UFO masters, Glyphs and VFB.

It copies Bitmap, SVG and PDF, and exports PNG, SVG and PDF.

FontLab Pad 2 runs on macOS 10.15 and newer and on Windows 10 and newer. It’s free to use on any number of devices belonging to one household or organization.
