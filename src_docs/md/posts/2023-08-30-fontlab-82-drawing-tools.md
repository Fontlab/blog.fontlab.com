FontLab 8.2 refines the drawing and contour toolset: improved Stroke and Brush modulation, new Harmonize Handles for G2 smoothness, smarter Knife slicing, and a redesigned drag-to-zoom experience. Whether you work with calligraphic strokes on the Sketchboard or precision nodes in the Glyph window, the tools are faster and more responsive.

<!-- more -->

## Draft and draw.

Sketch freely on the unlimited *Sketchboard* canvas, or dive straight into drafting separate glyphs and icons. Copy-paste or import SVG, PDF and bitmaps, use them natively, or convert to editable vectors with customizable autotracing. Explore expressive calligraphy with live *Power Stroke* and pressure-sensitive *Power Brush*. Draw with the uniquely efficient *Rapid* and traditional *Pen* tools, with the versatile *Pencil*, and with precise geometric tools — all in pixel-perfect integer coordinates or ultra-high fractional precision.

**FontLab 8.2** improves *Stroke* modulation and lets you snap *Stroke* and *Brush* thicknesses to distances, stems, or guides. On the *Sketchboard*, you can view segment and handle lengths and angles.

## Edit and refine.

Vector editing in FontLab is optimized for typeface design: from traditional to innovative. Reshape curves, splice contours and fill segments with *Contour*, *Knife*, and *Fill* tools. Drag points while others follow intelligently with *Nudge* and *Power Nudge*. Move nodes precisely without zooming in using the *Lever*. FontLab's editing tools keep the work flowing.

**FontLab 8.2** brings new *Harmonize Handles* for G2 smoothness, a redesigned drag-to-zoom experience, node sliding with arrow keys, a redesigned *View* menu, separate *Undo* for selection and deselection, better *Knife* slicing on nodes, easy selection of all Tunni lines, and easier conversion between smooth and corner nodes.

## Consistency and precision in every glyph.

Work on upright or slanted grids using flexible guidelines, measurement tools, and smart snapping. See lengths, angles, curve tension, stem thickness, and whitespace distance as you draw. Snap dynamically to zones, guides, hints, nodes, angles, stem distances, continuation lines, perpendicular lines, and centerlines. Scale, rotate, and slant losslessly with fractional precision.
