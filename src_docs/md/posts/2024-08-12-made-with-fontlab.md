Designers and foundries have used FontLab to create typefaces in every style and for scripts around the world. A selection of notable fonts made with FontLab 7 or FontLab 8 — from high-resolution historical revivals to expressive variable fonts.

<!-- more -->

FontLab has been the tool of choice for professional typeface designers for decades. The catalogue spans every genre: corporate custom typefaces, retail display fonts, revival projects, variable fonts, color fonts, and everything between.

## A few highlights

**Graduate** by Eduardo Tunni (Tipo) is a variable font showing what modern font technology can do with a classic collegiate aesthetic. Tunni, who calls FontLab "the all-inclusive app for typographers," used FontLab's integrated drawing and variable font tools throughout.

**PT Sans** — one of the most widely deployed Cyrillic typefaces on the web — was designed by Alexandra Korolkova at ParaType. It covers Latin and Cyrillic in four styles and has been embedded in hundreds of millions of devices and websites.

**Kristal** by Eyal Holtzman (Bold Monday) is an extensive text family designed using FontLab "not only for font design but for other complex vector drawing." Holtzman notes that since version 7, FontLab "does a much better job than other major vector editing software."

Fonts from **Canada Type** — a foundry with one of the most extensive digital revival and original design catalogues in the industry — have been built in FontLab across its entire history. Patrick Griffin of Canada Type calls FontLab 8 "the best and most comprehensive font making and editing program ever made."

**Bliss** and **Corbel** by Jeremy Tankard (typography.net) are both FontLab productions. Corbel ships as a system font with Windows; Bliss is a widely used humanist sans-serif. Tankard on the drawing tools: "the flexibility continues to impress."

Work from **Newglyph** (Ian Party), **ReType Foundry** (Ramiro Espinoza), **Karandash type foundry** (Vassil Kateliev), and **robertstrauch.de** (Robert Strauch and Alexander Haberer) — including corporate typefaces for BMW Group and KUKA AG — has been produced in FontLab. California Type Foundry's entire Hi-res Font Series, which involves pixel-accurate digitization from high-resolution historical scans, depends on FontLab's precision and fractional coordinate support.

## The full gallery

The complete gallery of fonts made with FontLab — updated continuously — is at [fontlab.com/made-with-fontlab/](https://www.fontlab.com/made-with-fontlab/). It covers recent releases across world scripts: Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Devanagari, Hebrew, CJK, and more.
