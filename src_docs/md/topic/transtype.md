# TransType

TransType is our universal font converter. With TransType, you can easily convert your collection of legacy Adobe PostScript Type 1 fonts into rock-solid, high-quality modern OpenType fonts, and use them in any apps for years to come! You can batch-convert your OpenType and TrueType fonts into CSS @font-face web fonts (WOFF & EOT) and use them on websites. And you can organize and rename font families automatically and with easy drag-and-drop, create intermediate weights by blending two fonts together, and apply special effects for rounded corners, tracking, autokerning, slanting or distortion.
