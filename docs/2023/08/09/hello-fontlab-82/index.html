    Hello, FontLab 8.2!

    FontLab 8.2 delivers 250 improvements over FontLab 8.0, with major additions including OpenType MATH table support, designspace-aware feature generation, smarter component handling, and a redesigned Welcome screen. It consolidates the FontLab 8 generation and sets the foundation for further free updates.

    FontLab has been the go-to app for professional typeface designers: smooth and consistent curves, comfortable spacing and kerning, technically solid font exports in any format.

    Meet FontLab 8.2 ↗️

    FontLab 8.2 has 250 improvements over FontLab 8.0, 700 over FontLab 7, and 1,200 over FontLab VI. It's easier to learn, better in every area, and faster.

    Create. Develop. Complete. Deliver.¶

    FontLab 8 is an integrated font creation environment from start to finish — a cross-platform workspace for professional typeface designers, font engineers, and vector graphics designers.

    FontLab 8.2 has a more uniform and streamlined UI in dark and light mode, faster operation with large fonts, improved text and numeric fields, and lower memory consumption. You can assign the Enter key to keyboard shortcuts.
