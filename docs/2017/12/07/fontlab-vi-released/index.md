# FontLab VI is here

FontLab Studio 5 was a workhorse. It shipped in 2004, carried a generation of type designers through the OpenType revolution, and aged gracefully — right up until it didn't. On 7 December 2017, we replaced it.

**FontLab VI** is a ground-up rewrite for macOS and Windows: 64-bit, native, and built around a drawing model that reflects how type designers actually think. Here is what you get on day one:

- **Sketchboard** — a freeform canvas where you can sketch and develop letterforms before they become glyphs, combining vector and bitmap artwork in one place.
- **Smart corners** — a corner component system that lets you design a serif or ink trap once and apply it parametrically across an entire typeface; change the master and every glyph updates.
- **Point reduction and optimization** — clean up imported or traced outlines in one step; FontLab removes redundant nodes while preserving the curve's shape.
- **Color fonts** — full support for OpenType SVG, COLR/CPAL, and sbix color font formats, all editable directly in the glyph window.
- **Variable font design** — built-in multi-master editing with axis support, ready for the OpenType Font Variations (variable fonts) format that shipped in browsers and operating systems the same year.
- **Elements and auto layers** — a reusable component system that goes beyond traditional composites: elements carry their own outlines and can be scaled, rotated, or flipped independently per glyph.
- **FontAudit** — a live quality checker that flags potential problems (open contours, missing extrema, wrong direction) as you draw, without interrupting your workflow.

FontLab VI opens and exports UFO, VFB (FontLab Studio 5 format), OpenType PS and TT, Variable OpenType, SVG, and more. The 30-day trial is fully functional.
